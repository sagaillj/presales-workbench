# Workbench v0.5.0

A calmer daily workspace, clearer replies and a guided setup that learns how you work.

- Permanent compact header, aligned metrics, customer-work wording, a collapsible Schedule/Activity/Completed sidebar and clearer full-month goals.
- Separate reply box with Default, Concise and Detailed drafts; copy icon, linked deal names and Slack/Gmail shortcuts where verified recipient details exist.
- Immediate next-card promotion after Done, with Undo restoring the original card and unsaved notes.
- Ten Coaching and combined Metrics layouts, with independent saved choices. Coaching centers goals, weekly learning, next practice and attributed feedback. Metrics separates influenced MRR, Led + Assisted contribution, credited deals, calls and roadblocks without invented series or quota inference.
- Five colorways plus custom background, text, accent and font settings.
- Optional weekly or every-other-week feedback: choose a score and click Submit inside Workbench. Optional comments, email, time estimate and local counts go to the fixed pilot Google Form, with verified receipt and safe retries. A prefilled Google Form is available after a known service failure. Nothing submits in the background.
- Optional daily GitHub release checks while open, with a guided upgrade prompt that preserves your workspace and stops for conflicting local app edits.
- Expanded Codex-led setup: allow at least an hour for prerequisites, your own systems and permissions, call capture, writing voice, goals and a verified first run. Granola or Wispr Flow Notetaker are recommended capture options; Zoom/Gong transcripts can also supply evidence.

New installs start unconfigured. Demo examples remain separate from personal work. CRM writes, sends, source access and recurring schedules require the recipient’s own authorization. Hours saved and checkbox completions are self-reported, not verified delivered work or measured business lift.
